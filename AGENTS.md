- This resource is for anyone, independent of the owner's career history.
- Assume every technical term may be new to the learner.
- Teach foundations at the moment a scenario needs them. Never send the learner away to complete a prerequisite course before continuing.
- Blend explanation, diagrams, predictions, experiments, evidence and reflection into a coherent experience.
- Revisit ideas in progressively harder situations, including edge cases and trade-offs.
