# Review Checklist

Use this checklist during sub-agent review before commit or push.

- Is exactly one major topic taught?
- Are all required subtopics covered?
- Does the lesson start with a warm welcome and progress map?
- Are technical terms defined before first use?
- Was there a live research sweep with current sources?
- Are recent incidents, tools, trends, and debates woven into the teaching?
- Is the ending complete?
- Does the tone match the learner-safe mentoring style requested by the user?
- Are any claims obviously stale, weakly sourced, or too confident?
