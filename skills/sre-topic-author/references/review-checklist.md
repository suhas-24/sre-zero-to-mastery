# Independent Review Checklist

Use this checklist for scenario-led lessons and substantial learner-facing changes. Review the change in its real reading order.

## Entry and flow

- Can a newcomer enter this page without earlier lessons and understand the goal?
- Does the situation make clear why each idea is introduced?
- Are connected concepts blended where the problem needs them, while focused reference material remains easy to find?
- Do prediction, evidence, experiment and explanation form a coherent line of reasoning?

## Definitions and accuracy

- Is each unfamiliar term explained before the learner must use it?
- Did the review include diagram labels, code, charts, captions and controls, not only prose?
- Do examples, units and diagrams match the real mechanism?
- Are assumptions, simplifications, version-sensitive claims and disputed guidance clear?

## Edge cases and actions

- Does at least one meaningful case challenge a tempting assumption?
- Are plausible alternative causes distinguished by observable evidence?
- Are action risks, limits and recovery checks explained?
- Can the learner tell what remains uncertain?

## Coverage and delivery

- Does the mapped coverage match what the page actually teaches?
- Are deferred concepts, source gaps and unrun labs labeled honestly?
- Are sources traceable and authoritative for the claims they support?
- Are media accessible and consistent, including text alternatives, captions, keyboard use and motion needs where relevant?
- Are the checks proportionate to the change, and is the result recorded?
