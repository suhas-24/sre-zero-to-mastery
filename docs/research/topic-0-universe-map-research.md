# Topic 0 Research Notes

## Purpose

Ground the initial "Complete SRE Universe Map" in current industry practice before Topic 1 begins.

## Research Highlights

- Google's SRE books remain the canonical conceptual foundation for the discipline.
- DORA research still strongly influences hiring and team-performance conversations, especially around delivery performance, reliability, and feedback loops.
- OpenTelemetry has effectively become the default open standard for telemetry instrumentation across modern cloud-native systems.
- eBPF has moved from niche kernel magic to mainstream observability, profiling, networking, and security discussion.
- Platform engineering has become a major adjacent career track and is increasingly paired with SRE responsibilities.
- AI-assisted operations is moving from alert summarization toward guided triage, runbook generation, and cautious remediation support, but fully autonomous SRE remains opinionated and not universally trusted.
- Kubernetes, Linux, networking, Terraform, observability, incident response, and cloud fundamentals remain the strongest hire-signal areas for 2025-2026.

## Current Industry Direction

- "Classic ops" knowledge is still necessary, but employers now expect stronger software, platform, and cloud-native depth.
- Reliability work is shifting left into paved-road platform tooling, policy automation, and developer self-service.
- AI is reducing triage time most effectively where telemetry, runbooks, and dependency graphs are already clean.
- Teams still debate how much SRE should own versus enable: embedded SRE, platform SRE, and enablement models all coexist.
